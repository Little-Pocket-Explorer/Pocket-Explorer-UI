This update improves Profile settings and card sharing.

- Child profile, Discovery preferences, Privacy, Location and Parent controls open distinct settings pages. Changes require the family PIN.
- Open a card's share button to send it to a friend, publish its approximate location, or copy a public link.
- Open event sharing from the event's top-right share button, copy its link, or search for a friend and send the invitation.
- Browse friend activity and shared cards, send messages, and offer a card when requesting an exchange.
- Open Discovery Quizzes from Map or Collection. Fresh discoveries can be practiced with Quiz me now.
- Inspect Story, Knowledge, Versions and Location in card details.

Please test settings changes after reopening the app, sharing a card with a friend, copying its link and returning from the share sheet. Also check large text, your preferred language and the complete discovery journey.
