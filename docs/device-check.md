# Pocket Explorer: Five-minute iPhone check

TestFlight 0.1.0 (14) is available in Hackathon Internal. Update directly in TestFlight to preserve the existing journal.

## Try the main journey

1. Open the app. Choose a language on first use. Later, use the avatar at the top right of Home, then Language.
2. Tap the microphone at the bottom right of Home and ask a simple question, such as Why is the sky blue?
3. Stop recording, check that the last words remain in the transcript, then tap the upward arrow to send.
4. Listen to the answer. English uses Emma Dragon HD and Simplified Chinese uses Xiaoxiao gentle. After listening, enable airplane mode and replay that answer to check the local cache, then restore connectivity. Leaving the page while audio is playing or waiting should stop it.
5. Tap the button to make a card. The words should save first. Waiting for the picture should not prevent further exploration. Leave and return to check its state.
6. Tap Map in the bottom tabs and open the new record in the adventure list below the map. It appears in the list even without a saved location. Tap Finish adventure & make a memory, or Play this memory if one already exists. Pause, next and replay should be easy to find. Longer text should allow more reading time.

One new question and card are enough. Reuse that record for the remaining checks.

## Check sharing

1. Open a card or trip sharing preview and review the words that will become public.
2. Tap Create a sharing link. If it is still waiting, close and reopen the preview. It should retain the same operation and the original selected content.
3. Copy the link and open it in iPhone Safari. Check the card, picture and memory.
4. Return to the app and stop sharing the story. If it is still waiting, leave and return. The old URL should disappear after completion.
5. Refresh the old Safari link. It should show that sharing has stopped rather than display the story.

You can complete this on your own phone without sending the link to someone else.

## Try three easily missed details

- Increase the system text size to an accessibility size. Published sharing actions should scroll, and longer labels such as Stop sharing this story should remain readable. Restore your preferred text size afterwards.
- Switch the interface language and reopen the original card. Controls can change language while the child's saved words retain their original language.
- Tap the camera at the bottom left of Home and photograph an ordinary object. If camera or microphone permission is declined, typing should remain available.

## Useful feedback

Record the page, the action you took, the actual result, interface language and iOS version. For narration, identify the language, answer and specific part that sounded stiff.
