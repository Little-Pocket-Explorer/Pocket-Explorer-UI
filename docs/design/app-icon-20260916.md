# Discovery cards app icon

The revised mark represents exploring the world and keeping discoveries as knowledge cards. A mint globe sits on a warm ivory collectible card, with a forest-green card behind it and one gold discovery sparkle.

- No language-specific text or letters.
- Tactile clay treatment follows the existing native illustration direction.
- The square master has an opaque full-bleed background. iOS applies the outer icon shape.
- The same mark appears in the home header and language welcome screen.
- Avatar choices and discovery artwork remain separate from the app brand.
