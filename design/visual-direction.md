# Visual direction

The approved baseline is [the concept](reference.html). The iPhone is the main experience. The public website carries the same visual language into a family story.

Use cream paper (#FFFDF4), forest green (#176D56), generous rounded typography, soft natural scenes and a restrained iridescent card frame. The collectible is the child's discovery, with their own words on its reverse. A reveal settles and stays still. Respect reduced motion.

Key implemented screens are captured in [key-screens](key-screens): world, voice-entry, card-front, card-reverse and memory. The share-preview and public website captures are included as well. Screenshots demonstrate rendering, not human approval.

The map uses Apple MapKit with sourced city coordinates. The compact overview does not pan so that vertical gestures scroll the journal. Its markers remain tappable. A separate trip list always keeps saved discoveries reachable.
